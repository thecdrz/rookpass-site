AES-256 Vault

Every entry is encrypted with a key derived from your master password via Argon2id. Nothing is stored in plaintext.

Face ID & Touch ID

Biometric unlock with your master password held in the Secure Enclave. Falls back gracefully to PIN.

AutoFill Everywhere

Fills passwords and passkeys in Safari and every app on iOS. Set RookPass as your Default Password App to also save new logins on form submit (iOS 26.2+).

Passkeys

Register and sign in with WebAuthn passkeys through the RookPass AutoFill extension. Passwordless, phishing-resistant.

Built-in Codes

Full TOTP authenticator with QR scanner, live countdown, and a home screen widget for your most-used codes.

Themes and Language

Six hand-tuned themes and an in-app language picker make RookPass feel personal without turning Settings into a design lab.

Password Health

Check weak, reused, and old passwords, plus individual breach checks, for free. Pro adds vault-wide breach scans and expiry reminders.

Rook Send · Pro

Share any vault entry as an encrypted, expiring link — no app required for the recipient. Key lives only in the URL fragment; our relay never sees it.

Structured Items

Store credit cards, identities, bank accounts, passports, driver's licenses, API credentials, and software licenses — not just passwords.

Import & Export

Import supported files from Apple Passwords, 1Password, Bitwarden, and more. Export encrypted .rook backups or CSV. Follow the switching guide.
